# CashFlow Pro — Personal & Business Cash Flow Tracker

A modern web application for end-to-end **Personal & Business** cash flow tracking, executive analytics, running balance ledger reconciliation, and **2-Way Google Sheets Sync**.

## Features

- **Dual Workspaces**: Switch seamlessly between **Personal**, **Business**, and **Combined** cash flow views.
- **Executive Dashboard**:
  - Real-time KPIs: Total Cash on Hand, Cleared Inflows, Cleared Outflows, Net Cash Flow, and Cash Flow Margin %.
  - Interactive Charts: Monthly Cash Inflow vs. Outflow & Net Surplus, Cumulative Cash Balance Trajectory, and Expense Breakdown by Category.
  - Pending Cash Pipeline (settle receivables & upcoming bills with 1 click) and Monthly Spend Guardrails.
- **End-to-End Cash Tracking**:
  - **Cash Flow Ledger**: Full transaction history with chronological **Running Cash Balance** after every cleared entry.
  - **Accounts & Wallets**: Manage Bank Checking, E-Wallets, Physical Cash, High-Yield Savings, Corporate Cards, and Tax Reserves.
  - **Direct-Method Cash Flow Statement**: Operating, Investing, and Financing cash flow reconciliation.
- **Google Sheets 2-Way Sync**:
  - Built-in Google Apps Script bridge (`Code.gs`) for 1-click Push/Pull sync with any Google Sheet, plus CSV Import & Export.

## Getting Started

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
